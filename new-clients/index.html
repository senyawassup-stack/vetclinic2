What to bring

What to bring, and why

Past recordsSo we know your pet's history, vaccines and past care.

A list of medicationsIncluding doses and supplements, so we can avoid overlaps.

Your questionsWrite them down, it is easy to forget them in the room.

A leash, carrier or secure containerKeeps your pet, and every other pet in the waiting room, safe and calm.

When to call right away

  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse, seizures, or unresponsiveness
  • Possible poisoning (a toxic plant, food or medication)
  • Severe injury, heavy bleeding, or being hit by a car
  • Repeated vomiting, a swollen hard belly, or straining to urinate (especially male cats)
  • A rabbit that stops eating or producing droppings
  • A reptile that is limp, unresponsive, or has a prolapse

This list isn't complete. If you're worried, call us. If we're closed, contact the nearest 24-hour emergency hospital.

FAQ

Good questions

How do I book?

Call (224) 261-8470 or send an appointment request through our contact page. We will call to confirm a time.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Saturday 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM, closed Sunday.

Do you see exotic pets?

We see select species, including rabbits and reptiles. Call us and we'll tell you if we can help with others.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Past records, a list of medications, and your questions. Bring cats in carriers, dogs on leashes and exotics in a secure container.

What if my pet has an emergency?

Call us right away at (224) 261-8470. If we're closed, contact the nearest 24-hour emergency animal hospital.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Please call us for payment details.

Can I get medical advice through the website or email?

No. Please call us. This website isn't for medical advice.

Where are you located and where can I park?

3113 Dundee Road, Northbrook, IL 60062. Please call us for parking details.
