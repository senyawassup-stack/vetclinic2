Privacy Policy

Who we are

Kohn Animal Hospital, 3113 Dundee Road, Northbrook, IL 60062. Phone (224) 261-8470, email hello@kohnanimalhospital.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms (name, phone, optional email, pet details, note) and standard hosting logs. We use cookies or similar storage only with your consent, plus one saved setting that remembers your privacy choice.

Why

To answer your appointment request, reply to you and keep the site running.

Who receives it

Our hosting provider and email provider. An embedded map (Google) loads only if you allow it.

Keeping your data

We keep requests only as long as needed to respond and for normal record-keeping.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information. Call or email us.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service is perfectly secure. Please do not send medical details.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Global Privacy Control

We honor Global Privacy Control and treat it as a rejection of optional features.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date below.
